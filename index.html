Week 1 · Introduction L1–3

  • Ecology = study of interactions that determine distribution and abundance ★ · oikos = household, logos = study ★
  • Approaches: theoretical (models) · laboratory (experiments ★) · field (direct observation ★)
  • Theophrastus: Enquiry into Plants, father of botany ★ · Linnaeus: modern taxonomy ★ · Humboldt: biogeography ★ · Malthus: population ecology ★ · Darwin: natural selection ★
  • Haeckel ne "ecology" coin kiya · Spencer ne "survival of the fittest" · Tansley ne ecosystem concept diya · Elton = father of animal ecology · Odum = Fundamentals of Ecology · Vernadsky = biogeochemical cycles
  • Genetic adaptation = inheritable fitness ★ · selection types: directional, stabilizing (birth weight), disruptive

Week 2 · Structure & biodiversity L4–6

  • Hierarchical structures stable ★ hote hain · emergent: whole > sum of parts ★
  • Cell = smallest unit ★ · tissue = similar cells + ECM ★ · organ system ★ · sabse upar biosphere
  • α = within ecosystem ★ · β = among ecosystems · γ = among geographies · richness = number of species ★
  • Gene polymorphic hai agar most common allele < 95% · hotspot = high richness + endemism + threat
  • Use values: direct, indirect, option · non-use ★: existence, altruistic, bequest (future generations) ★
  • Simpson D = 1/ΣPᵢ² · Shannon H = −ΣPᵢ ln Pᵢ

Week 3 · Interactions L7–9

  • Competition (−,−) ★ · amensalism (0,−) · commensalism (+,0) · protocooperation (+,+ non-obligatory: birds on giraffe) ★ · mutualism (+,+ obligatory)
  • Exploitative = scramble ★ (resource consume karna) · interference = contest ★ (rokna, fight) · apparent = common predator ke through
  • Leech = ectoparasite ★ · Plasmodium = endoparasite ★ · ethology = animal behaviour ki study ★
  • Allo-grooming = doosre ko groom karna ★ · penguin spacing = territorial ★ · Hamilton rB > C

Week 4 · Energetics L10–12

  • Tree → birds → parasites → hyperparasites = inverted pyramid of numbers ★ · energy pyramid hamesha upright
  • GPP = photosynthesis se fix energy per time ★ · NPP = GPP − respiration ★ · compensation point: P = R
  • Glacial lake = oligotrophic ★ · detritus chain fallen leaves se shuru ★ · decomposer = dead material → soil ★
  • 10% rule, upar jaate hue energy kam hoti hai ★ · plants ~1% · Haber process → nitrogen cycle ★ · primary nutrients N, P, K

Week 5 · Population L13–15

  • Additions = natality + immigration ★ · replacement fertility > 2 ★
  • Cover board → herpetofauna ★ · pan trap → pollinators ★ · pelt count = relative density (absolute nahi) ★
  • Simple random: har combination ka equal chance ★ · systematic: har kth unit ★ · sampling frame = list of units ★
  • Capture–recapture ka first stage = capture, mark, release ★ · logistic curve = S-shaped ★

Week 6 · Community L16–18

  • Guild = similar resource use ★ · ecotone = transition zone ★ · abundance = number, biomass, cover ★
  • IVI = RD + RF + RDo ★ · fundamental ≥ realised niche ★ · CDI = (y₁+y₂)/y · IS = 2c/(a+b)
  • Resistance = bina badle tikna ★ · resilience = badal ke wapas aana
  • Disclimax = cattle/man ★ · lithosere ⊂ xerosere ★ · secondary = disturbance ke baad ★ · autogenic = organisms khud ★

Week 7 · Distribution L19–21

  • Estuary = fresh + saline ka meeting zone ★ (high/intermediate/low salinity) · mangroves = high salinity + inundation ★
  • Pneumatophores = hawa ke liye upar ugti roots ★ · raat ki neeche aati thandi hawa = hill wind ★ · flamingos = food + safe breeding ★

Week 8 · Threatened species L22–24

Week 9 · Human ecology L25–27

  • Anthropocene ★ · I = P × A × T · demographic transition: high–high → low–low ★

  • 3 pillars: environmental, economic, social ★ (trans-boundary nahi) · clean tech ka theme: water purification
  • Stalactite = roof se neeche ★ · stalagmite = floor se upar · dono mil ke column

Week 10 · Ecology of change L28–30

  • Biocontrol ka risk: predator khud pest ban sakta hai ★ (mongoose) · ecotoxicology: population → biosphere levels ★

Numericals · saare formulas

Course mein numericals mainly L13–L14 (population), L5 (diversity indices), L10–L11 (energy), L16/L18 (community) aur L22 (species-area) se hain. Quiz mein yeh aaye the: species-area (z = 0.3), replacement fertility, importance value aur R + G = M + F. Saare numbers maine code chala ke check kiye hain.

1 · Capture–recapture (Lincoln–Petersen) L13, L14

m / n = M / N  ⇒  N = (M × n) / m

M = pehli baar mark kiye gaye animals · n = second sample mein kitne pakde · m = second sample mein marked kitne the · N = total population

Lecture example (striped hyena, camera traps): pehle sample mein 50 photos, second mein 50 photos, jinme 20 pehle wale individuals the.
N = 50 × 50 / 20 = 125 hyenas. Natural body marks (tiger stripes, lion whisker/vibrissae pattern, hyena stripes, chital spots) "mark" ka kaam karte hain.
Assumptions: marked animals randomly mix ho jaate hain aur marks girte nahi. Removal method ki assumptions alag hain: no immigration/emigration, no birth/death, aur trapped animals permanently remove kiye jaate hain. Capture–recapture ka first stage = capture, mark and release ★

2 · Population change L13, L35

N(t+1) = N(t) + B + I − D − E

Additions = natality + immigration ★. Losses = mortality + emigration. Natural increase = B − D. Net migration = I − E.

N = 500, B = 60, I = 20, D = 40, E = 10
N(t+1) = 500 + 60 + 20 − 40 − 10 = 530

Rates "per 1000" mein hote hain: CBR = annual live births per 1000 individuals; CDR = deaths per 1000; GFR = births per 1000 females of reproductive age; ASFR = births per 1000 females of a specific age class; TFR = average births per female over her reproductive life; juvenile mortality = juvenile deaths per 1000 live births.

Population 1500 hai aur saal mein 45 births hue. CBR?
45 / 1500 × 1000 = 30 per 1000
Replacement level fertility: population ko grow karne ke liye TFR 2 se zyada hona chahiye ★, kyunki 1 male + 1 female ko replace karne ke liye 2 offspring chahiye.

3 · Geometric growth with R₀ L14

N(t+1) = R₀ · N(t)    R₀ = N(t+1) / N(t)

R₀ > 1 ka matlab population badh rahi hai, R₀ < 1 ghat rahi hai, aur R₀ = 1 constant hai. Lecture example: 5 billion se 7 billion hua to R₀ = 7/5 = 1.4.

R₀ = 1.5, N₀ = 10
Gen 1 = 15 · Gen 2 = 22.5 · Gen 3 = 33.75 · Gen 9 ≈ 384 (exponential, J-shaped)

4 · Logistic growth L14, L31

dN/dt = r · N · (K − N) / K

Iska graph S-shaped (sigmoid) ★ hota hai, jisme teen phases hain: lag → log → stationary. Population K (carrying capacity) ke paas pahunchti hai to growth slow ho jaati hai ★. Sabse tez growth N = K/2 pe hoti hai, aur wahi MSY ka point hai ★.

Lecture example: K = 100, N = 25, maximum addition dN/dt = 10. r kya hoga?
10 = r × 25 × (100 − 25)/100 ⇒ r = 10 × 100 / (25 × 75) = 0.533
K = 500, N = 100, dN/dt = 40
r = 40 × 500 / (100 × 400) = 0.5
MSY: N = K/2 pe dN/dt = rK/4. Agar r = 0.4 aur K = 1000 hai:
MSY = 0.4 × 1000 / 4 = 100 per unit time

5 · Sustainable yield (fishery) L31, L36

S₂ = S₁ + R + G − M − F  ⇒ sustainable (S₂ = S₁): R + G = M + F

R = recruitment (naye individuals) · G = growth of survivors · M = natural mortality · F = yield to fishery ★ (fishing se nikaala gaya total weight)

R = 20 t, G = 30 t, M = 15 t. Sustainable catch F kitna?
F = R + G − M = 35 t
Ludwig's ratchet ke hisaab se harvesting rate hamesha badhta hai ★. Achhe saal mein log zyada pakadte hain, aur bure saal mein subsidy maangte hain.

6 · Species–area relation L22, L36

S = c · A^z  ⇒  S₂ / S₁ = (A₂ / A₁)^z
Quiz ★: area 90% kam ho gaya (A₂ = 0.1 A₁) aur z = 0.30 hai.
S₂/S₁ = 0.1^0.3 = 0.501 ⇒ half species bachti hain, 1/10 nahi.
z = 0.15 lene pe: 0.1^0.15 = 0.708, yaani ~29% species loss.
Lecture: tropical forest ~1.8% per year kam ho raha hai, z = 0.15 lo to ~0.27% species per year jaati hain. 10 million species ka 0.27% ≈ 27,000 species per year.

7 · Diversity indices L5

Pᵢ = nᵢ / N  ·  Simpson D = 1 / Σ Pᵢ²  ·  E = D / D_max
Shannon H = − Σ Pᵢ · ln Pᵢ  ·  E = H / H_max  (H_max = ln S)
Trap: is course mein Simpson index ka version D = 1/ΣPᵢ² padhaya gaya hai (inverse Simpson). Kai books 1 − ΣPᵢ² use karti hain. Exam mein is course ka version use karna. Shannon mein log natural log (ln, base e) hai. D_max tab hota hai jab saari species equal hon, aur tab D_max = S.
3 species, counts 50, 30, 20 (N = 100)
P = 0.5, 0.3, 0.2 · ΣP² = 0.25 + 0.09 + 0.04 = 0.38 ⇒ D = 2.63, E = 2.63/3 = 0.88
H = −(0.5 ln 0.5 + 0.3 ln 0.3 + 0.2 ln 0.2) = 1.03, H_max = ln 3 = 1.099, E = 0.94
4 equal species ke liye: D = 4, H = ln 4 = 1.386

8 · Community: RD, RF, RDo, IVI L16

RD = n(x)/Σn × 100 · RF = freq(x)/Σfreq × 100 · RDo = basal area(x)/Σ basal area × 100
Importance Value (IVI) = RD + RF + RDo  (max = 300) ★
10 quadrats hain. A: 60 trees, 8 quadrats mein present, basal area 30 · B: 30 trees, 6 quadrats, 15 · C: 10 trees, 2 quadrats, 5
SpRD %Freq %RF %RDo %IVI
A60.08050.060.0170.0
B30.06037.530.097.5
C10.02012.510.032.5

Frequency = (jin quadrats mein species mili / total quadrats) × 100. RF = 80/160 × 100 = 50.

9 · Community Dominance Index & Index of Similarity L16, L18

CDI = (y₁ + y₂) / y × 100   ·   IS = 2c / (a + b)

y₁, y₂ = do sabse abundant species · y = total abundance · a, b = dono communities mein species ki ginti · c = dono mein common species

y₁ = 40, y₂ = 30, total = 100
CDI = 70%
a = 20, b = 30, c = 10
IS = 20 / 50 = 0.4

10 · Energy flow & efficiencies L10, L11

Exploitation = Iₙ/Pₙ₋₁ · Assimilation = Aₙ/Iₙ · Net production = Pₙ/Aₙ · Gross production = Pₙ/Iₙ
Ecological efficiency = (Iₙ/Pₙ₋₁)(Aₙ/Iₙ)(Pₙ/Aₙ) = Pₙ/Pₙ₋₁
Lecture example: Kanha mein chital har saal 10,000 kg biomass produce karte hain, aur tiger usme se 100 kg khata hai.
Exploitation efficiency = 100/10000 = 1%
10% rule: sunlight 100,000 J → grass 1,000 J (plants ~1%) → herbivore 100 J → carnivore 10 J → top carnivore 1 J.
Har transfer pe 10% aage jaata hai aur 90% respiration aur incomplete digestion mein lose hota hai. Food chain mein upar jaate hue energy kam hoti hai ★
NPP = GPP − Respiration ★  ·  NPP = APAR × LUE  ·  Productivity = production / time
GPP = 40 cal, R = 15 cal ⇒ NPP = 25 cal. Plant ne 1000 cal intercept kiye aur 40 cal fix kiye ⇒ gross efficiency = 4%. 1 ton biomass 2 saal mein ban gaya ⇒ productivity = 0.5 t/yr.

11 · Doubling time & Hamilton's rule L26, L9

P(t) = P₀ e^(kt)  ⇒  t_d = ln 2 / k ≈ 0.693 / k
k = 0.02 per year ⇒ t_d = 0.693/0.02 = 34.7 years
Kin selection favoured when r · B > C
r: sibling 0.5 · nephew 0.25 · cousin 0.125. Haldane ki line: "life for 2 siblings, 4 nephews or 8 cousins" break-even hai. 3 siblings bachaye to r × B = 0.5 × 3 = 1.5 > 1 (apni life), yaani trait favoured hai.

12 · Sampling statistics L14

mean μ = Σx/n · SD σ = √(Σ(x−μ)²/n) · SE = σ/√n · Range = max − min · CV = σ/μ × 100%
Corbett chital group sizes. Core zone: mean 25.2, σ = 1.249, SE = 0.395, range 4, CV ≈ 5%. Buffer zone: mean 20.7, σ = 11.385, SE = 3.6, range 37, CV ≈ 55%.
Buffer zone fragmented hai aur wahan human influence zyada hai, isliye group sizes mein variation zyada hai. Sambar density example: 18 beats ka sum 101 tha, to 101/18 = 5.61 per km².
Line transect: perpendicular distance = d·sinθ (d = animal tak ki distance, θ = angle). Precision ka matlab measurements ek doosre ke paas hain. Accuracy ka matlab measurements true value ke paas hain. Agar method precise hai par accurate nahi, to use calibrate karna padta hai.
